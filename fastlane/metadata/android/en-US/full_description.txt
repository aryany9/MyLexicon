<p><b>My Lexicon</b> is a powerful <b>open-source alternative for "Create Dictionary" application</b>. Designed specifically for Android, it serves as an offline-first personal knowledge companion where you can create, organize, and master words, quotes, phrases, idioms, personal notes, tags, favorites, and custom collections.</p>
<p>The app keeps all your data stored 100% locally on your Android device — offering a privacy-respecting, lightweight solution to building your custom dictionary without relying on any cloud backend.</p>
